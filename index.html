The learning rule is the one you'd follow by hand. If the needle points the wrong way, turn the knob of every switched-on input, plus the bias, one step toward the correct side. Training keeps going until each example swings the needle past the minimum on its correct side. On Auto the minimum is 2 × the step size, so a bigger step aims for a bigger swing. Choosing a new example set resets the knobs to 0.

Circuit values

The ends of each pot go to +V and −V and the wiper gives −V…+V. When its switch is on, the wiper feeds the shared summing node through its own resistor. The meter runs from that node to the battery's center tap. A high meter resistance shrinks the needle swing but doesn't flip its sign.
